**Windows:** installed apps update themselves to this version. To install from scratch, download `APC-Universal-Repair-Setup-0.8.3.exe`, or the portable exe to run without installing.

**Android:** the installed app says when this version is out and opens its APK, `apc-universal-repair-0.8.3.apk`. To install from scratch, open that file on the phone.

### What's new
- **The vehicle lists come filled in** (#468): the item form's vehicle brands and models start with the 30 brands most sold in Brazil, such as Fiat, Volkswagen, Chevrolet, Hyundai and Toyota, and Brazilian classics such as Gurgel, Puma and DKW, with 299 of their usual models, old and new. The brands and models you already added stay, with their spellings. The full list is in `docs/vehicle-lists.md`.
- **One brand under one name** (#468): a brand typed under another name, such as "GM", "VW" or "M.Benz", becomes Chevrolet, Volkswagen or Mercedes-Benz. When both names were in use, their models and parts come together under one, and no part is lost.

### Novidades
- **As listas de veículos já vêm preenchidas** (#468): as marcas e modelos de veículo do formulário do item começam com as 30 marcas mais vendidas no Brasil, como Fiat, Volkswagen, Chevrolet, Hyundai e Toyota, e clássicos brasileiros como Gurgel, Puma e DKW, com 299 dos modelos de costume delas, antigos e novos. As marcas e modelos que você já cadastrou ficam, com a grafia deles. A lista completa está em `docs/vehicle-lists.md`.
- **Uma marca com um nome só** (#468): uma marca digitada com outro nome, como "GM", "VW" ou "M.Benz", vira Chevrolet, Volkswagen ou Mercedes-Benz. Quando os dois nomes estavam em uso, os modelos e as peças deles se juntam num só, e nenhuma peça se perde.
